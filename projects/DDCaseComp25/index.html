2025 Diamond Dollars Case Competition

Baseball Analytics

In this project, I competed in the 2025 Diamond Dollars Case Competition, where student teams presented innovative solutions to challenges faced by Major League Baseball front offices. Our case centered on analyzing relief pitcher performance and developing new statistical measures to better evaluate how effective a reliever truly is, beyond traditional metrics like ERA or saves.

My team’s process involved examining detailed game‑level data, including leverage situations, inherited runners, and multi‑inning usage, to identify gaps in existing evaluation methods. We engineered new performance indicators that accounted for context, consistency, and impact on team outcomes, while also comparing them against established sabermetric benchmarks. This required both rigorous statistical modeling and a deep understanding of bullpen strategy in modern baseball.

The final presentation was delivered to MLB executives and industry professionals, highlighting how our proposed metrics could improve contract valuation, roster construction, and bullpen management. This project underscored the importance of blending quantitative analysis with baseball operations insight, demonstrating how innovative statistics can reshape how teams measure and deploy relief pitchers.
