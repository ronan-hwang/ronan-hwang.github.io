Ganance Activity Classifier Model

Data Science

In this project, I developed a machine learning model that leveraged smartwatch sensor data to classify human movements and activities. The goal was to explore how embedded sensors could capture motion patterns and translate them into meaningful activity recognition, laying the groundwork for applications in fitness tracking, health monitoring, and wearable technology.

The process involved generating data through prototype devices equipped with embedded sensors such as the LI3MDL magnetometer and LSM6DSOX accelerometer/gyroscope. I tested sensor performance, designed preprocessing pipelines, and engineered features to capture movement signatures. The classification model was trained and evaluated on this dataset, with iterative improvements to optimize accuracy and robustness across different activity types.

Beyond model development, I collaborated on presentation materials and technical documentation to communicate findings and progress to the team. This project highlighted the importance of combining hardware experimentation with machine learning, demonstrating how sensor‑driven data can be transformed into actionable insights for wearable technology solutions.
