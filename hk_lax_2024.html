Zag Tours "Hong Kong Lacrosse Open 2024"

Business Strategy and Sports Planning

In this project, I developed a comprehensive 10‑day itinerary for Princeton’s Women’s Lacrosse team to participate in the Hong Kong Lacrosse Open 2024. Working with Zag Tours, a sports travel group that emphasizes cultural immersion, I focused on balancing athletic scheduling with meaningful local experiences.

The planning process required evaluating hotels, transportation, and pricing while also considering cultural factors such as visas, dining, and sightseeing opportunities. By integrating these elements, the itinerary aimed to provide athletes not only with smooth logistics but also with a deeper appreciation of Hong Kong’s culture during their stay.

The final itinerary was presented to the founders of Zag Tours, showcasing how sports travel can extend beyond competition to create distinctive cultural experiences. This project highlighted the importance of combining operational detail with cultural awareness in international athletic tours.
